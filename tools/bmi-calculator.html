What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight. It applies to adult men and women and is widely used as a simple screening tool for weight categories. While BMI doesn't directly measure body fat, it correlates with more direct measures of body fat.

BMI Categories

  • Underweight: BMI less than 18.5
  • Normal weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25 to 29.9
  • Obesity: BMI 30 or greater

Note: BMI is a screening tool and does not directly assess body composition. Consult a healthcare provider for medical advice.
