How Salary Conversion Works

Salary conversions use your work schedule as the basis. Hourly rate × hours per week = weekly pay. Weekly × 52 = annual. Monthly is annual ÷ 12. Biweekly is weekly × 2 (26 pay periods/year). Adjust hours/week for part-time work or overtime, and weeks/year to subtract vacation time.
