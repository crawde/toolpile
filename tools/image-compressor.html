Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I compress?

JPEG images can typically be compressed 50-80% with minimal quality loss at the 75% quality setting. WebP format offers even better compression — typically 25-35% smaller than JPEG at the same quality. PNG compression is lossless and less dramatic. The actual savings depend on the image content and original compression level.

Will I lose image quality?

JPEG and WebP use lossy compression — some quality is sacrificed for smaller file sizes. At 75% quality, the difference is nearly imperceptible for most images. You can compare the original and compressed versions side-by-side in this tool. PNG compression is lossless.

Is there a file size or count limit?

No hard limits — everything runs in your browser. However, very large images (>50MP) or many images at once may be slow depending on your device. Each image is processed independently.

Can I resize images too?

Yes! Set a maximum width in the controls. Images wider than the max will be proportionally scaled down. Set to 0 or leave empty for no resizing.
