Binary vs Decimal Units

In binary (used by computers), 1 KB = 1,024 bytes (2^10). In decimal (used by storage manufacturers), 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. This is why a "500 GB" hard drive shows ~465 GB in your OS. IEC standards use KiB/MiB/GiB for binary and KB/MB/GB for decimal, but most people use KB/MB/GB for both. This tool lets you toggle between both conventions.
