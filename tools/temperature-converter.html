Temperature Conversion Formulas

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32. °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. K = °C + 273.15. Celsius and Fahrenheit scales intersect at −40° (−40°C = −40°F). Kelvin is the SI unit — 0 K is absolute zero, the lowest possible temperature where all molecular motion ceases.
