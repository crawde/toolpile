How Roman Numerals Work

Roman numerals use seven symbols: I(1), V(5), X(10), L(50), C(100), D(500), M(1000). Numbers are formed by combining symbols and adding values. When a smaller symbol precedes a larger one, it's subtracted (IV=4, IX=9). The standard system covers 1–3,999. Year 2024 = MMXXIV (1000+1000+10+10+4).
